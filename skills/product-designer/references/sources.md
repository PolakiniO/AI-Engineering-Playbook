## Accessibility references

The W3C Understanding documents explain individual criteria; they are not a complete conformance audit:

- [Contrast minimum, SC 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html): text contrast thresholds and exceptions.
- [Reflow, SC 1.4.10](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html): narrow viewport equivalents and two-dimensional content exceptions.
- [Target size minimum, SC 2.5.8](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html): pointer target sizing and documented exceptions.

Apply the adopting project's accessibility requirements and consult the full relevant standard where needed. The quality gates also contain general design and review guidance, not solely requirements derived from these three criteria.

## Reuse boundary

Before importing any external component or asset, record its actual source, inspected revision/date, applicable license or permission, required notices, dependency/network behavior, and proposed publication scope. Unknown rights remain unresolved, not implicitly approved. Keep proprietary or private material outside public examples and distributions. Prefer original implementations of transferable interaction patterns when direct reuse is inappropriate.
